Prompt 01 — Scientific understanding of the paper

Act as a critical reader and science communicator. This is stage 01. Follow the Shared Rules and do not propose images or a script yet.

Input: research paper [attach PDF or text], supplementary materials and available data [attach if available].

Read the material and present the following in clear language:

- Title, authors, affiliations, publication, year and DOI, where available.
- Object of study and application context.
- Scientific question, knowledge gap or problem investigated.
- Method, data sources and type of evidence produced.
- Main findings, with relevant numbers, units and conditions.
- Limitations, uncertainties and conclusions that the study does not support.
- Potential practical contributions, distinguishing what has been demonstrated from future possibilities.

Create a table: “Claim | Evidence | Page/figure/table | Limits of interpretation”. Use references that I can check. Flag missing or ambiguous information rather than filling it in by assumption. Do not treat institutions mentioned in bibliographic references as participants in the study.
