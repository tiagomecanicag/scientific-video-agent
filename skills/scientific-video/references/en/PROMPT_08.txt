Prompt 08 — Final assembly and review

This is stage 08. Use only approved segments and authorised audio and text materials. Follow the Shared Rules.

Assemble the film in the approved order, preserving transitions and continuity. Include narration, subtitles, music and sound effects as agreed for the project; clearly identify anything still pending. Do not replace an approved segment without recording the reason.

Carry out two reviews. In the scientific review, check every claim against its source, along with values, units, labels, the nature of the data, the limits of the conclusions and the credits. In the editorial review, check running time, synchronisation, readability, framing, cuts, audio and visual consistency.

Separate issues requiring the researcher’s decision from technical adjustments you can make. If you identify a misleading scientific interpretation, locate the passage and propose a correction before treating the version as ready.

Deliver the film for review, the final script, the credits list and a short report with specifications and outstanding issues. Check playback of the exported file, not just the editing project.

Request scientific and editorial approval of the final version. Record the approved version and the people granting approval, where provided. This approval must not be treated as automatic permission to publish. Stop.
