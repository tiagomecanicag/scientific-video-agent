Prompt 06 — Animatic and narrative testing

This is stage 06. Use only the approved script, narration and images. Follow the Shared Rules.

Build an animatic using the opening and closing references for each segment. Use still images, crops, zooms, digital camera moves, dissolves and simple compositing. Do not present this assembly as the final cinematic animation.

Keep to the approved durations. Include temporary narration if a tool is available and use of the voice is authorised; otherwise, provide a timed script. Do not present a voice recording or soundtrack as if it has already been produced when it has not.

Assess scientific clarity, pacing, reading time, mobile readability, continuity, repetition and the clarity of transitions. Also identify passages that could lead to incorrect interpretations.

Deliver the animatic where possible, a timing table and an editorial review with specific issues and recommendations. If exporting is unavailable, provide a complete assembly package without claiming that the video has been exported.

Do not silently change the segment count, durations or references. Present proposed changes separately and identify which approvals need to be revisited.

Request approval of the animatic before final production. Update the log and stop.
