Prompt 09 — Communication package and publication approval

This is stage 09. Use the approved final version and follow the Shared Rules. Prepare the publication materials, but do not publish or send messages on your own initiative.

Create a package containing the final file in the approved format, a cover image, a public-facing title, a brief description, the full paper reference, the supplied DOI or link, credits and available subtitles. Retain relevant scientific caveats without overloading the text.

Adapt the language to the approved audience and channel. Do not add promotional claims that go beyond the paper. Do not treat an organisation’s participation in the study as institutional endorsement of the video.

Organise sources, visual references, segments, audio, editing files, exports and documentation. Include a manifest recording the origin of the materials, final versions, approvals and any outstanding permissions reported by the user. Preserve originals and do not expose private information or internal materials in the public package.

Deliver the files and texts ready for review. Provide a short list of what will be shared, on which channel and with which credits. Request explicit authorisation for that specific publication; if no publishing tool is available, provide instructions for manual submission.

Complete the Continuity Log and record the actual status: “ready for review”, “approved for publication” or “published”, only when supported by corresponding evidence.
